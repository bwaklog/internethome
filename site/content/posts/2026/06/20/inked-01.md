Bought a membership for [rootconf](https://hasgeek.com/rootconf), got the chance to attend the tropical edition on databases. Sat for some interesting talks like incremental computation for database by Mihai Budiu, a talk by Shomik Ghose who is a database engineer at Antithesis and talked about PBTs at scale for an OLAP database that they rolled out ([pangolin](https://antithesis.com/blog/2025/testing_pangolin/)). Been waiting to get a membership for 2 years and decided to put in my first salary in this. Really looking forward to the future events (there is an LSM tree workshop coming up the next Saturday)

![](https://images.adihegde.com/blogs/inked-1/rootconf.jpg)
Rootconf at TERI Auditorium

Ended the week by meeting up with my friends during the Bengaluru systems meet-up, which made me realise how insanely good people are. Rohith Suresh took us over understanding stack traces, how we can still get a good stack trace with `-fomit-frame-pointer` and gdb’d his way through the entire demo. I’ve always known of how powerful gdb was, but today I witnessed it being used to another extreme. I could not keep up with maintaining notes for this meet up (just was in awe watching the entire demo + explanation of his exploratory work), but here are some things I came across. But not a match for the 1 hour walkthrough on stack traces. 
- Stack walking: space and time trade-offs [maskray.me](https://maskray.me/blog/2025-10-26-stack-walking-space-and-time-trade-offs)
- Getting the call stack without a frame pointer [yosefk.com](https://yosefk.com/cgi-bin/comments.cgi?post=blog/getting-the-call-stack-without-a-frame-pointer)
- Stack unwinding [maskray.me](https://maskray.me/blog/2020-11-08-stack-unwinding)

Work is great so far, spent the last week meeting up with the entire team in the 6 monthly team meet up (no one comes on site to work since covid). Besides my main tasks at work, during breaks I spend some time just going through some hidden confluence documents on some of their architectures and other things in general. Have definitely exploited the mini snacks counter in my floor so far. In a way, the office being empty gives a nice environment to get work done. 

I’ve been spoilt by good coffee. Essentially went from having coffee for the sake of _staying awake_ and now purely for the taste of it. All began when I stole a good cup of americano from my sister @ The Old House, now I’m pretty much beyond the point of no return. Have only had my coffee black since the start of 2026.
