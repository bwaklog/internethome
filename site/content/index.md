# Aditya Hegde

Hello world! I'm Aditya Hegde. I'll be interning at Akamai this Summer. Prev. research intern at [PES Innovation Lab](https://www.theinnovationlab.in/) (prev. Microsoft Innovation Lab). 

Currently pursuing my bachelors degree in CSE at [PESU](https://pes.edu). I have a fond interest in building systems and storage. Outside of my work, I spend my time _trying_ to play the Guitar, am a "hobby photographer" and love rock.

Vintage at Mels Drive In, Universal Studios Singapore

I'm part of a small community called [HSP](https://hsp-ec.xyz) in my university and together run a the FOSS team [homebrew](https://homebrew.hsp-ec.xyz) where we try to grow and network beyond our university in different meetups and workshops. Since my internship at PIL (PES Innovation Lab), I am part of the research lab as a member where we maintain the infra for events and mentor the incomming intern batches.

This site came about after my frustrations of maintaining a simple webpage without moving to a JS framework. A couple of my friends and I built [anna](https://github.com/anna-ssg/anna), which is what is used to build this site.

This site has a bunch of semi-organised [posts](/collections/post.html) that I try writing once in a while and TIL's (huge inspiration from Simon Willson's TIL page at [til.simonwillison.net](https://til.simonwillison.net/)), where I will be dumping links and other side notes that I take while learning and when I attend talks. I have a work in progress [linkstash](linkstash.adihegde.com) which scrapes my notes from obsidian.
